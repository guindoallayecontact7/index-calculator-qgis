about=Index Calculator Pro - Agricultural Geospatial Intelligence System.
      Written and maintained by Allaye GUINDO.
      GitHub: https://github.com/guindoallayecontact7/index-calculator-qgis

      The whole workflow stays in one window: fetch a scene for a zone, check the bands, compute the indices, then look at the fields. Everything runs on your own machine. The only network use is the optional scene download from public STAC catalogues, and it happens only when you click Search or Download.

      HOW TO USE:
      1. Image: download a scene for your area (optional), or drop a folder of Sentinel-2 or Landsat bands (.jp2 or .tif). The sensor is detected from the file names.
      2. Settings: check the resampling resolution and method, cloud masking, automatic correction and the output folder.
      3. Indices: pick indices one by one, by category or with a preset.
      4. Click Generate indices. Layers are styled and grouped by category.

      SPECTRAL LIBRARY (80 indices):
      Vegetation (27): NDVI, EVI, EVI2, SAVI, GNDVI, NDRE, MSAVI, MSAVI2, OSAVI, RDVI, VARI, TGI, GLI, MTVI2, RVI, DVI, IPVI, NGRDI, GRVI, TVI, ATSAVI, TNDVI, GEMI, PVI, MSR, ARVI, MCARI2.
      Chlorophyll (6): ClRE, MTCI, RENDVI, NDRE2, CVI, LCI.
      Water (12): NDWI, NDMI, LSWI, MSI, MNDWI, WRI, AWEI, SWI, NMDI, GVMI, NWI, MBWI.
      Soil (11): BSI, RI, NDTI, CI, GSAVI, TSAVI, EEVI, SOCI, SI, BI, ALBEDO.
      Stress (8): NDSI, PRI, CRI, ARI, SIPI, PSRI, DSWI, RVSI.
      Crop (8): LAI, CCCI, GCVI, WDRVI, MCARI, TCARI, IRECI, S2REP.
      Burn (4): NBR, NBR2, BAI, MIRBI.
      Built-up (4): NDBI, UI, IBI, EBBI.

      WORKSPACE FEATURES:
      - Three numbered steps: Image, Settings, Indices, with a status line
      - Online search and area-limited download (Sentinel-2 L2A, Landsat 4-9 C2 L2), clipped to the boundary polygon
      - Quick index filter and category presets
      - Automatic band mapping for Sentinel-2 and Landsat 4-9 (TM, ETM+, OLI/TIRS)
      - Resampling to 10 m, 30 m or a custom resolution
      - Cloud masking with SCL (Sentinel-2) or QA_PIXEL (Landsat)
      - Colour ramps from the actual raster values, layers grouped by category
      - Formula viewer with formula, bands and interpretation
      - Boundary clipping for SHP, GeoJSON and GPKG
      - Quicklook preview of the selected product before download
      - Optional automatic correction: digital numbers to reflectance, haze removal and low-contrast rescaling

      SUPPORTED FILE FORMATS:
      .jp2 (Sentinel-2 individual bands), .tif/.tiff (Landsat or multi-band), .img, .vrt

      REQUIREMENTS:
      - QGIS 3.16 or higher (including QGIS 4.x)
      - GDAL (included with QGIS by default)
      - Internet connection only for the optional online scene download

      PRIVACY AND NETWORK:
      - No account, no telemetry, no credential storage.
      - Network access happens only when Search products or Download product is clicked.
      - All scene requests use HTTPS from public STAC catalogues.
      - All other processing runs locally on the user machine.
